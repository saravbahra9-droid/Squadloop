Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what we collect on squadloop.ae, why we collect it, who handles it for us and the choices you have. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

IWho we are

squadloop.ae is run by Kuki&CO LLC, trading as SquadLoop (“SquadLoop”, “we”, “us”), a company licensed in the Sharjah Media City (Shams) free zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates (licence no. 2649634.01).

For anything about your personal data, write to hello@squadloop.ae.

IIWhat this policy covers

This policy covers this website, including its waitlist form and its parent feedback form. SquadLoop is not yet launched. When the SquadLoop app opens, it will have its own privacy notice covering accounts, bookings and payments.

IIIWhat we collect

  • Waitlist form: whether you are a parent, an academy or provider, or a school; your name; your email address; and anything you choose to write in the message box.
  • Parent feedback form: your answers to the questions (how many children do activities, how many activities a week, how you book today, whether you would use one app), your area, and, only if you give them, your email address and a comment.
  • Link details: if you arrive through a link we shared with you, a short label showing which link it was (for example “academy”).
  • Visit statistics: general information about visits to the site, such as the pages viewed, the site that referred you, your country, and your type of device and browser.

We do not ask for children’s names, dates of birth, schools or any other details about children on this website. Please do not include them in the message boxes.

IVWhy we use it

  • To add you to the waitlist and write to you about early access and launch.
  • To reply when you ask us something.
  • To understand what families, academies and schools need, so we build the right product.
  • To see how the website is used and improve it.

We use the details you send us because you have agreed to it by submitting the form. You can change your mind at any time by writing to us, and we will stop.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

VIHow long we keep it

We keep waitlist and feedback details while the waitlist is running and we are preparing to launch, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

VIIYour choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you and send you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your details;
  • stop or limit how we use them;
  • take you off the waitlist and stop writing to you.

Write to hello@squadloop.ae from the email address you signed up with and we will act on it as soon as we reasonably can.

These rights are available under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and may also be available under other laws that apply to you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can raise a concern with the UAE Data Office.

VIIIHow we protect it

  • The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • The waitlist database accepts new entries from the website but cannot be read by the public.
  • Only the SquadLoop team can see the details you send.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to keep your details safe and will tell you if something goes wrong that affects you.

IXChildren

This website is for adults: parents and guardians, academies and other activity providers, and schools. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website.

XChanges to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell the people on the waitlist by email.
